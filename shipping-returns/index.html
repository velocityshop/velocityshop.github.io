Shipping, returns and condition

The short version: pieces leave within 1-2 business days, you have 30 days to send one back, and every listing shows the piece as it really is.

Shipping

We ship to addresses in Canada and the United States. Orders are folded, packed and handed to the carrier within 1-2 business days of checkout. Shipping options and costs are shown on our Square store before you pay, so the total is never a surprise.

Returns

If a piece is not right, you can return it within 30 days of delivery. It needs to be unworn and in the condition it arrived in. To start a return, reach us through our Square store (opens in a new tab) ↗ with your order details.

Condition

Everything we sell is pre-owned. Each piece is checked by hand before it is listed: seams, zips, cuffs, collars and fabric. If there is wear, a mark or a flaw, it is shown in the photos and named in the listing, so what you see is what arrives. Tested electronics say so in their titles.

Checkout and payment

This site is a window onto the shop. When you choose a piece, you check out on our Square store, which handles payment securely. Your card details go to Square and never to us. All prices are in Canadian dollars.
