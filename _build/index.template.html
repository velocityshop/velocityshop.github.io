Velocity Shop is a small resale shop for pre-loved clothing and the odd find, from Polo Ralph Lauren to Pokémon cards.

Expect Lacoste polos, Raptors and Blue Jays gear, band and tour tees, Levi's and chinos, mugs and tested vintage electronics. Almost everything is one of a kind. Each piece is checked, photographed and listed by us, and when it sells, it is gone. New pieces go up most weeks; the latest went up on {{NEWEST}}.

Everything here is a single piece, so if it is your size, it is yours.
