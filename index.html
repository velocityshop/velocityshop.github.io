Shipping & returns
Where do you ship, and how fast?

We ship to Canada and the United States. Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1-2 business days of checkout. Shipping options and costs are shown on our Square store before you pay.

Can I return something?

Yes. You can return a piece within 30 days of delivery, as long as it is unworn and in the condition it arrived in. Start a return through our Square store with your order details.

What condition are the pieces in?

Everything is pre-owned. Each piece is checked by hand before it is listed, and any wear, mark or flaw is shown in the photos and named in the listing.

How do sizes work?
