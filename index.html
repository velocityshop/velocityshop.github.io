    About Velocity Shop

    We sell pre-loved clothing and the odd collectible: polos, team tees, hoodies, jackets and jeans with plenty of wear left in them.

    Almost everything we stock is one of a kind. When a piece sells, it is gone, so each one gets its own photos, its real size and its price on the tag before it goes up.

    You will find names like Polo Ralph Lauren, Lacoste, Champion, Levi's and Uniqlo UT hanging next to Raptors, Blue Jays and Argonauts gear. New pieces go up most days.

    Buying second-hand keeps good clothes in use for longer, and it means you get something nobody else is wearing. That is the whole idea.
